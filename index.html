Pablo Neruda

The Chilean poet Pablo Neruda (born Neftali Ricardo Reyes Basoalto) was one of the most influential writers of the 20th century. He won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1971, served as a Chilean diplomat and politician, lived mainly in Santiago, Temuco, and his three iconic houses in Chile (as well as several countries for his consular missions), and died on September 23, 1973, in Santiago, Chile.
